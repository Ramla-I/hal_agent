Two questions, not one

Validity is a fact about the manual and our reading of it: a constraint rejected at collection, one whose quoted sentence could not be tied to the register it constrains, or one the closed-book judge read against the datasheet and declined. Applicability is a fact about the chip crate and the code generator: a rule can be perfectly true and still have nowhere to land, because the peripheral is absent from this part or its grammar kind has no emitter yet.

Reporting them as one number would blame extraction for what is really coverage. 3,009 of the 8,250 constraints that survived validity were never wrong — they had nowhere to go.
