The previous agent's retrieval parameters mainly relied on keyword search with limited pages after the keyword, no table removal, and a moderate embedding count. Given the substantial gaps in register and peripheral coverage, especially for peripherals and registers only in the SVD but missing from the retrieved context, it's clear the current keyword search parameters are insufficient to capture the diversity of register descriptions in the manual.

The manual content indicates systematic naming variations, detailed register bit descriptions, and hierarchical register addresses, which are not always explicitly tied to surface-level keywords. Therefore, increasing the breadth of context retrieval is essential.

To improve coverage, I propose:
- Switching to semantic search to better handle naming conventions and paraphrased descriptions.
- Increasing the number of embeddings to 50 to diversify context understanding.
- Raising pages_after_keyword to 8 or 10 to include more related content.
- Maintaining query rewriting to account for naming variants.
- Keeping remove_tables as true, given that formatted tables may obscure the register details, but ensure filtering doesn't truncate critical info.
- Reintroducing re-ranking to optimize relevant results.
- Slightly lowering score_threshold to 0.2 to accept more borderline relevant contexts.

These adjustments aim to capture more peripheral references, implicit descriptions, and varied naming, especially for peripherals and registers missing in the previous context.
